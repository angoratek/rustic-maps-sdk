Watch the Android, iOS and React Native reference applications run the same
task, recorded on the three targets and played back in your browser. Choose a
platform, then move through the screens at your own pace.

## What the walkthrough shows

Six screens per platform, all captured from one revision:

1. The plan sheet with both endpoints chosen.
2. The provider section, showing each capability's display name and credential
   state.
3. Simulated navigation at the first instruction, with the turn banner, route
   polyline and ETA line.
4. The styled vector base with its place labels.
5. The layers control closed over the base map.
6. The same camera with the layers panel open on its lower rows.

The three recordings share one frame, so the platforms stay in step.

## Play it

1. Open the walkthrough.
2. Choose Android, iOS or React Native.
3. Use the screen dots, the arrow keys or a swipe to move through the frames.

The player runs entirely in your browser. It needs no account, no session and
no provider credential, and every frame is served from this site.

## Prefer the live demo?

The [live wasm demo](demo/) runs the same SDK in your browser: pan and zoom a
map, plan a recorded route and follow a simulated trip. It also works without
a provider key.
